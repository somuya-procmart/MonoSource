●FAQ

What buyers ask us first

Do we have to change our SAP?

No. MonoSource connects to SAP, Ariba, Oracle and Ivalua. Your approvals and POs stay where they are.

Do we lose our current suppliers?

No. Your contract suppliers stay. We make sure every plant uses them.

What if a supplier lets us down?

We source from our network of 10,000+ vendors and hold stock in 30+ warehouses. You still see one order.

How do we save money?

Plants buy at contract price, every item has one price, and fewer vendors means fewer POs and invoices.

Is our data safe?

We sign an NDA, give role-based access, and use your data only for your business.
